 The newer openers follow the same rules: \"Would you consider selling [street] at some point? If not, just say no and I won't text again.\" (default), \"I saw [street] came off the market. Still hoping to sell, or keeping it for now?\" (expired listing, keeping it is a no), \"Saw [street] is listed by owner. Is it still available or already sold?\" (for sale by owner, still available counts as open to selling and already sold is a no), \"Trying to reach whoever handles the property at [street]. Is that you? If not, sorry to bother you.\" (affidavit of death, same as the affidavit opener), and the one follow up \"If selling isn't on your mind, just say no and I'll close my file.\" (same as the default). The opener \"Are you still the owner of [street]? If not, no worries.\" is an ownership opener: follow OWNERSHIP OPENER above, not this section.
